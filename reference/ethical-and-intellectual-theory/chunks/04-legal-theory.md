### Legal Theory

Source: `legal-theory/README.md`

This directory is the **entry point for legal-theory research and terminology** in *Ethical & Intellectual Theory*. It groups separately documented concepts without requiring a new link on the repository homepage each time a theory is added.

The entries are **author-defined research concepts and diagnostic vocabulary**, not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal analysis, independent validation, or legal advice. Listing an entry does not establish that its thesis is legally correct or that a proposed mechanism has been implemented.

#### Terminology and concepts

##### Law, ethics, and adversarial conduct

- **Legal Crime** — Something morally or ethically repulsive and abhorrent, but legal. A distinction between legal permissibility and moral judgment; **not an assertion of a criminal offense**.
- **Proper Construction Attack** — An attack carried through legal and lawful means. An analytical term; **not proof that a particular action is lawful, harmless, or authorized**.

##### Legal reasoning and inherited justification

- **Condition Capture and Defeat** — Four diagnostic patterns (**eliminated-condition capture, inherited-condition defeat, scope capture, reflexive condition defeat**) concerning necessary supporting conditions, inherited justification, scope, and internal contradiction. Research vocabulary; **not established legal doctrines**.

##### Knowledge antithesis and rejection concepts

- **Knowledge Antithesis** — Canonical name for the condition previously termed **Knowledge Corruption** and, earlier, **Anti-Knowledge**: material presented as knowledge compromised against reasoned examination or in relation to theory adoption or repudiation; the proposed root of Poison Cache.
- **Poison Cache** — Named concept explicitly rooted in Knowledge Antithesis; further definition and mechanism remain unresolved.
- **Designed-for-Rejection** — Proposed mechanism by which material is made to be rejected. Knowledge Antithesis may be part of it, but is not equivalent; historical abbreviation ICHK is not expanded.
- **Recursive Poisoning** — Proposed examination problem of encountering compromised material to understand material knowledge or Knowledge Antithesis; not established as universally necessary or safe.

#### Research archives and synthesis

- **Integrity of Legal Reasoning** — Disclosure-limited overview of the umbrella research program. **Bellotti Ladder** is preserved within it as a proposition-level recursive precedent and justificatory-dependency audit rather than treated only as a retired codename. The integrated architecture records its relationship to Condition Capture and Defeat, Implication, multidimensional attestation, governance, controlled findings, and unresolved validation obligations. The architecture also records four successor proposals on repair and finite reporting, first recorded on 27 November 2026: No Naked Implication, Minimal Surviving Proposition, Burden-Bounded Repair, and Potentially Unbounded Recursion, Finitely Reported. The public glossary provides concise explanations with their proposed status. Underlying procedures and detailed research remain outside the public overview.

- **Confounding the Court** - Research synthesis on open-world risk, transformative observation, adversarial purpose capture, and defensive reflexive inversion. Includes physical pages 1-14 of the 16 August 2026 archive, ending before the conversation transcript; the proposed framework remains a research theory.

#### Working method and evidence boundaries

The underlying legal-system research distinguishes **logical integrity**, **dependency/support integrity**, **operative legal authority**, **constitutional conformity**, **rights impact**, and **governance decisions**. A failure of supporting justification is not automatically proof that a conclusion is false; independent supporting routes must be considered.

Legal validity, moral or ethical judgment, political incentives, and technical feasibility are separate questions. All particular claims of unlawfulness, liability, or legal duty require applicable law and facts. Research classification alone creates no legal authorization or enforcement authority.

### Condition Capture and Defeat — Legal Reasoning Diagnostics

Source: `legal-theory/condition-capture-and-defeat/README.md`

**Status:** Author-defined research terminology; not an established legal doctrine, a judicial determination, or a finding of unlawful conduct.  
**Attribution:** Human-directed conceptual reframing and decomposition; AI-assisted editorial formulation under human instruction.

##### Object of examination: a condition

A **condition** is a premise, qualification, scope limit, rule requirement, or inherited supporting proposition on which a particular legal justification materially depends. The term is analytical; it does not necessarily mean a legally defined *condition precedent* or any person giving testimony.

The relevant questions are whether the condition is necessary to the justificatory route, whether it still holds within the claimed scope, and whether adequate independent support remains if it does not.

##### Two mechanisms

- **Condition capture:** A conclusion or later argument retains, transfers, or enlarges the justificatory force attributed to a condition beyond the scope or support that condition can presently supply. "Capture" names the reasoning defect; it does **not** by itself establish intent, coercion, wrongdoing, or a factual seizure of evidence.
- **Condition defeat:** A necessary supporting condition is removed, contradicted, rendered inapplicable, or otherwise fails for the claimed justificatory route. "Defeat" concerns that support route, **not** automatic falsity of the conclusion, loss of all legal authority, or a finding that a law is invalid.

Capture and defeat can coexist. A defeated condition may continue to be invoked as if it still authorizes the same conclusion; conversely, a narrowed condition may be overextended without having been wholly defeated.

##### Four diagnostic patterns

| Pattern                               | Classification           | Research definition                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|---------------------------------------|--------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Eliminated-condition capture (EC)** | Capture following defeat | An argument discards or defeats a condition necessary to its earlier justification but preserves the dependent conclusion as though that support survived, without adequate independent replacement. |
| **Inherited-condition defeat (ID)**   | Transmitted defeat       | A later decision or argument relies on an inherited supporting condition or conclusion after its necessary justification has already failed, without independently repairing the dependency.         |
| **Scope capture (SC)**                | Capture by overextension | A materially limited, narrowed, or weakened condition is invoked as though it still supports its former broader scope or authority.                                                                  |
| **Reflexive condition defeat (RD)**   | Internal defeat          | One operative argument both requires and rejects the same condition under materially equivalent circumstances, without a valid distinction, temporal qualification, or independent replacement.      |

These patterns describe analytically different failures. Their labels do not establish that the supporting condition was legally necessary in any particular case; that must be shown.

##### Examination procedure

1.  Identify the conclusion being supported and the exact **condition** asserted to support it.
2.  Specify the condition's source, relevant facts, temporal and legal scope, and whether it was **necessary** rather than merely associated with the conclusion.
3.  Identify the alleged capture or defeat: elimination, inherited failure, scope expansion, or internally inconsistent reliance.
4.  Test for adequate **independent replacement justification**, intervening changes, distinctions, and alternative supporting routes.
5.  Report the bounded outcome: `CAPTURE_INDICATED`, `CONDITION_DEFEATED_FOR_ROUTE`, `INDEPENDENT_SUPPORT_SURVIVES`, or `UNRESOLVED`. A classification alone must not be treated as a final legal determination.

##### Dependency and authority boundaries

**Dependency rule:** Failure of a supporting condition does **not** prove the dependent conclusion false. A conclusion may survive through another valid justification, a narrower construction, or a different source of legal authority.

Maintain separate assessments of logical validity, dependency integrity, operative authority, constitutional conformity, rights impact, and governance judgment. Distinguish an observed reasoning defect from allegations of misconduct, proof of intent, or grounds for relief.

**No intent inference:** "Capture" and "defeat" are diagnostic descriptions of an argument's treatment of conditions, not findings that a person deliberately manipulated evidence or committed an offense.

**Legal limit:** This vocabulary does not substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal research, factual evidence, or professional legal analysis. No enforcement authority or legal consequence arises from assigning a classification.

Part I - Executive synthesis

Core thesis
The work develops a general theory of decision-making under open-world uncertainty in which
probability estimates, observations, observers, validation procedures, and recovery mechanisms may all
be incomplete or adversarially manipulated. Its legal application treats a court as a coupled epistemic
and coercive institution: it receives transformed representations of events, classifies them through rules,
decides under mandatory burdens, and converts its conclusion into enforceable authority.

Central contribution The strongest original move is the transition from ordinary deception to purpose
capture: an attacker can cause a system to execute and legitimize the attacker's objective by making a
malicious alteration appear to improve the system's own agenda.

• Risk assessment already combines likelihood, impact, threat characteristics, vulnerabilities, and

• Information-hazard theory recognizes that acquiring or disseminating information can itself create

• Adversarial machine-learning research recognizes evasion, poisoning, abuse, trojans, backdoors,
and other attacks selected to exploit a model rather than sampled randomly from its ordinary
operating distribution. [S5]
• Reflexive-control literature closely parallels agenda capture: specially shaped information can
induce a target to make a decision favorable to the actor. [S6]
• Specification gaming and reward tampering provide a technical analogy for replacing an intended
objective with a manipulable proxy while preserving the appearance of success. [S7]
• Federal evidence doctrine distinguishes authentication, reliability, admissibility, and weight;
current rulemaking discussions about AI evidence explicitly examine validation, inputs,
auditability, independent evaluation, and notice. [S9][S10]

What remains proposed or unverified
• Probability does not become mathematically irrelevant merely because harm is large. The justified
conclusion is that high or irreversible harm lowers the probability threshold for investigation and
precaution, especially when the estimate is uncertain.
• Unknownness alone is not evidence of danger. A credible claim requires a mechanism, boundary
effect, anomaly, or other articulable basis.
• There is no general scientific basis in the sources reviewed for the claim that merely contemplating
an unspecified unknown necessarily degenerates the observer.
• 'Everything we know is wrong' is defensible as an operational audit posture - no premise is immune
from revision - but not as a literal universal truth claim.
• 'Confounding the court' was not identified in the reviewed authorities as an established federal
cause of action or doctrine. It is a proposed analytic category overlapping fraud on the court,
abuse of process, obstruction, candor duties, sanctions, and sham-litigation doctrine.

Part II - Theory progression

1. Severity-conditioned attention
As potential harm increases, increasingly low-probability edge cases require consideration. The point is
not that probability disappears, but that the permissible threshold for ignoring a risk changes with
severity, reversibility, uncertainty, and mitigation cost.

2. Unknown probability and open worlds
When the event space or probability model is incomplete, an unobserved case cannot safely be treated
as probability zero. The first task is to test the adequacy of the modeled domain.

3. False determinism from closed testing
Repeatedly testing only states admitted by the prevailing theory can produce uniform outcomes and
create an appearance of determinism while leaving unimagined or excluded states untested.

4. Domain-relative logic
Contraposition remains formally valid. Its limitation is epistemic: if both a proposition and its negation
are evaluated only inside an incomplete modeled domain, valid inference does not disclose what lies
outside that domain.

5. Transformative observation
Observation is a chain of interaction, transduction, processing, and classification. The target can change;
the signal can lose information; preprocessing can remove anomalies; categories can force an unknown
into a known label.

6. Coupled observer hazard
The observer is not only a sensor. Human cognition, instruments, software, institutions, and
interpretation form a coupled system whose state may change during inquiry.

7. Maliciously constructed observation
An adversary can design the object or record so that the expected act of inspection becomes the
delivery mechanism for misclassification, compromise, or cognitive manipulation.

8. Co-optable self-healing
Recovery remains necessary when interaction is unavoidable, but a repair process can restore toward a
corrupted baseline or learn from attacker-controlled evidence.

9. Agenda capture
The attacker need not defeat the target's agenda. The attacker can alter inputs, proxies, or apparent
improvements so the target voluntarily produces the attacker's result as though it were faithful
optimization.

10. Confounding the court
In law, agenda capture causes a court to use its own procedures, legitimacy, and coercive authority to
produce, validate, or preserve a substituted objective.

11. Lawful-means confounding
The attack may be compositional: each act can be permitted in isolation while the coordinated sequence
degrades the integrity of adjudication.

12. Defensive reflexive inversion
A defensively structured system can convert an attempted capture into containment, preserved
evidence, verifiable commitments, and an opportunity for independent attribution - without
manufacturing the wrongdoing.

Formal propositions
• Risk attention: Care(a) should increase with possible severity H, uncertainty U about the model,
irreversibility I, and adversarial selection A, and decrease only with credible probability evidence
and proportionate mitigation cost. This is a decision rule, not a claim that probability is irrelevant.
• Domain limitation: If P implies Q only over modeled domain Omega_M, then not-Q implies not-P
remains valid over Omega_M; it does not establish that Omega_M exhausts the real domain
Omega_R.

Where the work is strongest
• It treats the model boundary itself as a risk variable rather than assuming a complete state space.
• It recognizes targeted adversarial selection as categorically different from ordinary random
sampling.
• It models the human and machine observer as a coupled attack surface.
• It distinguishes preserving an unknown from accepting an interpretation of it.
• It notices that verification and self-healing can reproduce a compromised transformation or

• It identifies institutional legitimacy as an attack amplifier: a captured court does not merely err; it

• It identifies local legality/global distortion as a compositional problem that element-by-element

Where the work needs development
• Define the triggering threshold. High harm cannot justify unlimited investigation of every

• Protect legitimate advocacy, petitioning, access to courts, confidentiality, and due process.
• Develop falsifiers: state what evidence would count against a claim of confounding.
• Specify independent oversight so the anti-confounding mechanism cannot become a confounding
instrument.


Part IV - Application to law

Law as a forced-decision epistemic system
Courts cannot always suspend judgment until uncertainty disappears. They must decide under
jurisdictional rules, burdens of production and persuasion, evidentiary gates, deadlines, remedies,

standards of review, and finality. This makes law a strong application: residual uncertainty is not
eliminated; it is procedurally allocated.

The legal transformation chain can be stated as: historical event → perception → recording → collection
→ preservation → processing → legal classification → authentication → admissibility → factfinding →
judgment → enforcement. A defect can arise at any link, and repeating the terminal conclusion does not
independently verify the underlying event.

Critical distinctions Preservation is not authentication. Authentication is not reliability. Reliability is not
admissibility. Admissibility is not weight. Weight is not truth. A court-resistant framework must preserve
these separations.

Proposed definition: confounding the court
Confounding the court is the intentional or structural construction of evidence, procedure, legal framing,
institutional incentives, or review conditions that causes a court to produce, legitimize, or preserve a
substituted objective while perceiving that result as a faithful improvement in justice, accuracy, fairness,
efficiency, finality, safety, or another legitimate adjudicative purpose.

This definition is broader than ordinary deception because the attacker seeks not only a false belief but
the court's voluntary use of its own authority. It is broader than fraud on the court because the
proposed category also includes structurally confounding sequences in which no single representation is
necessarily false.

Proposed elements
1. An actor seeks a judicially or institutionally validated outcome.
2. The actor constructs or materially alters an input, representation, procedure, incentive, proxy,
sequence, or apparent decisional criterion.
3. The construction is designed to appear consistent with a legitimate adjudicative purpose.
4. The construction conceals or materially obscures the difference between that purpose and the
substituted objective.
5. It causes, or creates a substantial risk of causing, the court to use its process or authority to
produce, validate, or preserve the objective.
6. For intentional confounding, the actor acts knowingly or purposefully regarding that displacement.
Structural confounding does not require a single provable malicious actor.

Lawful-means confounding
The especially dangerous variant is compositional. Each filing, motion, objection, procedural election,
evidentiary selection, settlement position, or appeal can be formally available. The distortion appears

Part V - Defensive reflexive inversion

Definition
Defensive reflexive inversion structures unavoidable interaction so an attempted capture is contained
and converted into a preserved, independently testable record of the attempted operation. The attacker
seeks to make the system an instrument; the defense seeks to make the attack a probe that reveals the
attacker or vulnerability.

Boundary The trigger is not the conclusion. Trigger, attribution, culpability, and remedy are separate
determinations. A defensive mechanism that treats its own trigger as guilt can itself be spoofed, framed,
or captured.

Required safeguards
• Precommitment: neutral rules should exist before the dispute or target is selected.
• Noninducement: expose an existing disposition; do not manufacture it.
• Provenance: preserve originals, transformations, identities, timestamps, and custody.
• Isolation: keep suspect content from controlling the inspection environment.
• Semantic quarantine: preserve and characterize without prematurely accepting meaning.
• Heterogeneous verification: vary sensors, tools, analysts, pipelines, and starting assumptions.
• Separation of roles: the designer or beneficiary of a trigger cannot be its sole interpreter.
• Rebuttability: permit innocent explanations and adversarial testing.
• Reversibility and proportionality: use interim measures that can be corrected if attribution fails.
• Baseline integrity: self-healing must verify the state to which it restores.
• Independent audit: log enough of the process to reconstruct what occurred. [S8]
• Anti-capture review: test whether the defensive process is advancing its stated purpose or a
substituted one.

A court-facing review sequence
1. Identify the claimed legitimate objective and the specific decision requested.
2. Map the complete procedural and evidentiary sequence, not only isolated compliant acts.
3. Preserve the original record and disclose material transformations and dependencies.

#### Governing separations

The integrated research preserves the following distinctions:

``` text
logical pass != constitutional pass
loss of support for Q != negation of Q
logical result != governance decision
formally operative != logically well-supported
not certain risk != no risk
citation identity != proposition identity
analytical relevance != binding authority
```

An independent later justification can become a new root for a proposition. Earlier ancestry then ceases to be load-bearing beyond that re-anchoring point. Re-anchoring must be demonstrated from actual independent support.

A shared case name or opposite outcomes do not establish contradiction. The compared proposition and its material predicates must be equivalent, and no valid distinction or independent support may account for the difference.

#### Successor derivations: repair and finite reporting

The following propositions were derived on 27 November 2026 from the integrated architecture. They are successor theory, not archive-explicit findings, settled law, independently validated conclusions, or implemented operating rules.

##### TH-021 — No Naked Implication

When a historical premise fails or becomes unnecessary, releasing that substrate means removing it from active load-bearing service while preserving its provenance. It does not mean deleting the historical record.

A retained implication must not survive as a bare conclusion. It must remain attached to a typed record:

``` text
I* = <P, K, S, B, A, E>
```

- `P`: the proposition retained.
- `K`: its material conditions and predicates.
- `S`: its minimum surviving support, including any independent re-anchoring.
- `B`: its boundaries, exceptions, and known defeaters.
- `A`: its authority and formally operative status.
- `E`: its evidence and provenance.

An unknown field remains `UNKNOWN`; it is not silently omitted. Thus, `retained implication != unsupported conclusion`.

##### TH-022 — Minimal Surviving Proposition

After a dependency crack, the object of repair is not the strongest desired rule. It is the strongest proposition actually warranted by the surviving support, within the remaining scope and burden.

If several supported propositions are incomparable, the analysis reports each maximal candidate rather than selecting one without justification. Permissible outcomes include:

1.  The original proposition survives.
2.  An independently re-anchored proposition survives.
3.  Only a narrower proposition survives.
4.  Only a conditional or temporary proposition survives.
5.  No proposition presently survives.
6.  The result remains unresolved.

Loss of the original proposition's support does not itself establish its negation.

##### TH-023 — Burden-Bounded Repair

A repair derived from a surviving proposition must not exceed the scope, authority, or burden that the surviving support can carry. It must also not become more restrictive than the cracked rule it repairs. A more burdensome rule supported by genuinely new authority is a new root, not a repair attributed to the old rule.

Possible repairs include a narrower class or regulated act, an exception, an evidentiary threshold, a milestone, a review condition, an expiration point, a renewed-evidence requirement, or removal of the unsupported restriction.

Metrics inferred from the evidence are analytical implications. Metrics chosen for administration or policy are governance proposals. The two must remain separately labeled.

##### TH-024 — Potentially Unbounded Recursion, Finitely Reported

Dependency analysis may continue without a fixed theoretical depth, but each issued report must be finite and auditable. Every terminated branch records why it stopped.

Recognized stopping states include:

- demonstrated independent re-anchoring;
- immateriality to the proposition under examination;
- a detected cycle;
- a current authoritative root;
- an unavailable or unknown dependency;
- a declared research boundary; or
- exhaustion of the identified sources.

A stopping state closes the reported branch; it does not certify unseen ancestry. Recursion depth and report completeness are therefore distinct statuses.

These derivations extend the architecture's repair discipline without changing the production lock or the status of the frozen archives.

#### Typed doctrinal material

The research treats a doctrine under examination as a typed set that may include holdings, necessary justifications, materially relevant implications, contradictions or dependency failures, and express boundaries. Inclusion in the analytical set requires treatment; it does not give every item identical precedential authority.
